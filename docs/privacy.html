Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

hydRate is a free app for finding, rating, and adding public water fountains and bathrooms. It is made by Aiden Gonzalez ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. hydRate has no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your information.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address: used to sign in and to send password reset codes. It is never shown to other users.
  • Username: shown publicly next to the places, ratings, and photos you add.
  • Password: stored only as a salted, one-way hash. We can't see your password.

Things you contribute

Places you add (name, location, and details such as whether a fountain has a bottle filler), ratings, and photos are public: anyone using the app can see them, along with your username. Before a photo is uploaded, the app crops and resizes it and removes its metadata (including any GPS location, capture time, and camera details stored in the file).

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to center the map near you and to fetch nearby places. Your location is not stored on our servers or linked to your account. The only locations we store are the locations of places you choose to add.

Camera and photos

The app only accesses your camera or photo library when you choose to take or pick a photo of a place. Only the photo you confirm is uploaded.

Technical information

Like any internet service, our server receives your IP address with each request. We use it temporarily to prevent abuse (for example, limiting repeated login attempts) and in short-lived server logs. It is not used to identify or track you.

How we use information

  • To run the app: show places, ratings, and photos, and let you contribute.
  • To keep your account secure, including password resets.
  • To moderate content: when something is reported, we review the report and may remove content or accounts that break the Terms of Use.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Amazon Web Services (S3 and CloudFront) stores and delivers uploaded photos.
  • An email delivery provider sends password reset emails to your address.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map images. Your device downloads map tiles directly from OpenStreetMap's servers, which receive your IP address and the area of the map being viewed, as described in the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: open your Profile, tap the Settings (gear) icon, then Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • your account, email address, username, and password hash
  • all of your ratings
  • all of your photos
  • any reports you have filed

Places you added are kept on the map so other people can still find them, but they are no longer linked to you. Server database backups containing deleted data are overwritten within 14 days.

If you can't access the app, email us at the address below from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll do so within 30 days.

Children

hydRate is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
