## Full description (4000)

```
Thirsty? Need a restroom? hydRate shows you the water fountains and bathrooms around you, and how good they actually are.

FIND
• See fountains and bathrooms on a map centered on where you are
• Browse the closest spots in a list sorted by distance
• Check photos and details: bottle fillers, baby changing stations, sanitary products and more

RATE
• Fountains: taste, temperature and water pressure
• Bathrooms: cleanliness, privacy, decor, hand washing and drying
• Average scores help you pick the best spot nearby

CONTRIBUTE
• Add a missing fountain or bathroom in a few taps
• Snap a photo so others know what to look for
• Every place you add helps the next person out

PRIVACY FIRST
• No ads, no tracking
• Your location stays on your phone, and is only used to show what's nearby
• Photo location data is removed before upload
• Delete your account and data any time from Settings

hydRate is a free, community-built project. Spot something wrong or inappropriate? Report it right from the app.
```
